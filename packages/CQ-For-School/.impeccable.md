## Design Context

### Users
School administrators, HR leads, and department heads at international or culturally diverse schools. They use this product during working hours on desktop browsers to diagnose cultural misalignment between staff groups and take data-driven action. Context is professional, analytical, and time-pressured — they need clarity fast.

### Brand Personality
Professional, warm, data-driven. Three words: **clear, trustworthy, purposeful**. The product should feel like a smart, calm colleague who gives you the honest picture without alarm or ambiguity.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light mode. Warm off-white backgrounds (HSL 40 33% 98%). Teal primary (#2d9276 range), amber accent for warnings, red for critical states, green for positive. Typography: Fraunces for display headings, DM Sans for body. Cards use white bg with slate-200 borders and subtle shadow-sm. The product is institutional EdTech — refined, serious, no gimmicks.

### Design Principles
1. **Clarity before cleverness** — data must be legible at a glance; no decorative noise
2. **Semantic color** — red/amber/green carry consistent meaning throughout; no decorative color use
3. **Hierarchy through contrast** — use size and weight differences aggressively; avoid same-size-same-weight monotony  
4. **Purposeful density** — dashboards pack information efficiently but with enough breathing room to parse
5. **Consistency with siblings** — pages share header pattern (icon + h1 via font-display), card structure, and spacing conventions
